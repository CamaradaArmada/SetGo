# SetGo! 1.4.0

## New: BlessYou!
A new module with one button for your buffs. It shows what you're missing,
what your group is missing, and a buff back for anyone who buffs you. Left
click casts it. It uses group versions and greater blessings when they're
worth it, and paladins can set a blessing priority. There's also a party menu,
a macro you can drag to your bars, and an optional /thank. Type /blessyou or
open its settings in SetGo!.

## SetGo!
- **Damage Meter Reset Button** (Interface Tweaks): clears every session in
  one click.
- **Quick Menu:** buttons for Cooldown Manager, Click Casting and Quick
  Keybind Mode.
- **Share tracked quests:** shares one quest at a time and shows each
  member's answer. Click it again to stop.
- The minimap menu has shortcuts to your modules' windows.

## Speak!
- The pinned window has an options cog with text alignment and a Close
  button.
- Long links no longer get cut off, and the cursor is in the typing box as
  soon as the Board opens.

## Fetch!
- The keyring sits in the bag bar like a button, and right click only
  toggles the bar.
